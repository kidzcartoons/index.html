STORY

Bheem and friends are celebrating Achai Utsav as good deeds strengthen the three divine doors keeping Shaitaan trapped in Shaitaan Lok. Shaitaan needs the Sunhera Kamal from Padma Lok, Prithvi ka Dil from Hriday Lok and the Yin-Yang symbol from Yin-Yang Lok to break each door. Kirmada offers to get him the Sunhera Kamal while Zuhu offers to get him Prithvi ka Dil. Since Padma Lok is pure and only pure-hearted people can enter it, Kirmada sends a rot curse in Dholakpur. Dhooni Baba sends Bheem and friends to retrieve the Sunhera Kamal from Padma Lok to save Dholakpur. But when Bheem and friends return, Kirmada attacks them to get the Sunhera Kamal. Bheem and friends almost succeed in defeating Kirmada but Shaitaan intervenes and gets the Sunhera Kamal. Purity from the world vanishes and Padma Lok starts collapsing. Bheem and friends pray to Krishna who stabilizes Padma Lok and guides Bheem and friends to head to Hriday Lok and save the Prithvi ka Dil before Shaitaan gets his hands on it and love disappears from the world.
